The normal mode analysis of a system containing N vibrating nuclei in thermal equilibrium
leads to a 3N-multivariate normal distributino of nuclear displacements relative to their
equilibrium positions ($\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{R} - \mathbf{R}^0$):

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:Pu}
P(\mathbf{u}) = (2\pi)^{-3N/2} |\mathbf{U}|^{-1/2} \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2} \mathbf{u} \mathbf{U}^{-1} \mathbf{u}^T\right)
\end{equation}

where $\mathbf{u}^T$ represents the transpose of $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{U}$ is the covariance matrix of the displacements.

This covariance matrix is the  Mean Square Displacement Amplitude (MSDA) matrix associated with
the expectation values of the Cartesian nuclear displacement products (second moments):

$$
\mathbf{U} = \langle \mathbf{u} \mathbf{u}^T \rangle_T
$$

The temperature dependence of $\mathbf{U}$ is embedded in the MSDAs of the normal modes in its eigenvalues
$\mathbf{\delta} = (\delta_{i=1,3N})$:

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ULdL}
\mathbf{U} = \mathbf{L} \mathbf{\delta} \mathbf{L}^T
\end{equation}

where $\mathbf{L}$ is the matrix of eigenvectors (normal modes) of the Hessian matrix.

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:deltaj_def}
\delta_j = \frac{h}{8 \pi^2 \nu_j} \coth\left( \frac{h \nu_j}{2 k_B T} \right)
\end{equation}

where $\nu_j$ is the frequency of the $j$-th normal mode, $h$ is Planck's constant, and $k_B$ is Boltzmann's constant.

\subsection{MSDA implementation in AIDED}

In the \texttt{aided.io.vib.gaussian} module the Eigen‑representation
\eqref{eq:deltaj_def} is evaluated by the following fragment
\href{https://github.com/drjrm3/aided/blob/main/aided/io/vib/gaussian.py}
{\texttt{gaussian.py::GaussianLogReader.gen\_msda}}:
\begin{verbatim}
delta = ZPE_PREF_ANG2 / freqs / reduced_masses  # δ_j  in Å²
if temperature > 1e-9:
    mode_prefactor *= coth(THERM_FACTOR_CM_K * freqs / temperature)
    msda = np.einsum("is,s,js->ij", modes, delta, modes)
\end{verbatim}

\begin{itemize}

\item \texttt{ZPE\_PREF\_ANG2} implements $h/(8\pi^{2}c)$ together with
unit conversions (cm$^{-1}$$\to$s$^{-1}$ and amu$\to$\r{A}$^{2}$), so that the resulting
$\delta_j$ are directly in \r{A}$^{2}$.
\item \texttt{THERM\_FACTOR\_CM\_K}$:=h c/(2k_{\mathrm B})$ converts the argument of the
\texttt{coth} to dimensionless form.

\item The division by \texttt{reduced\_masses} in the prefactor converts the stored
\emph{mass‑weighted} eigenvectors \texttt{modes} into the Cartesian $\mathbf L$ used
in Eq. \eqref{eq:deltaj_def}.  Omitting this term would yield the mass‑weighted MSDA.

\item The Einstein summation
$U_{ij}=\texttt{modes}(is)\delta_s\texttt{modes}(js)$ realises
$\mathbf U=\mathbf L\boldsymbol\delta\mathbf L^{\mathsf T}$ efficiently.
\end{itemize}

Consequently, the MSDA matrix produced by the code is numerically identical to
Eq. \eqref{eq:deltaj_def} and all elements are expressed in Cartesian coordinates with units of
\r{A}$^{2}$.
